# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Just Nieman fans, event organizers, collaborators, and industry contacts visit the public site to discover music, upcoming appearances, and contact information. The artist uses the private admin surface to update that content quickly, often around releases and events.

## Product Purpose

The site is the authoritative home for Just Nieman online. It should make current music and appearances easy to find, give the artist a low-friction publishing workflow, and carry a distinctive identity without getting between visitors and the next action.

## Brand Personality

Electric, nocturnal, direct. The experience should feel confident and personal, with the focused energy of an intimate late-night set.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, neon-on-black crypto styling, glass-heavy interfaces, festival-poster clutter, and visual effects that obscure content or controls.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next action within reach.
2. Let music and artwork carry the personality.
3. Keep publishing calm, legible, and reversible.
4. Use energy with restraint so important states stay clear.
5. Make the smallest screen a first-class surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, meaningful image alternatives, semantic structure, and reduced-motion behavior. Maintain strong text and control contrast across dark surfaces.
